# MediQuery — Canada/Québec Buyer Outreach Strategy

## Objective

Run a focused strategic-acquisition campaign for the MediQuery software asset. The goal is to identify organizations that already operate healthcare software, clinical workflow products, laboratory/data platforms, or health-data infrastructure and could shorten their time-to-market by acquiring the codebase and engineering foundation.

## Priority buyer profiles

### Tier 1 — Direct strategic fit

Target companies with an existing medical-document, clinical-workflow, EMR, patient-portal, laboratory, or health-data product. Contact the **CEO/Founder, VP Product, VP Engineering/CTO, Head of AI/Data, or Corporate Development/M&A lead**.

### Tier 2 — Health IT platforms

Target established healthcare IT vendors where MediQuery could become a document-ingestion or evidence-preservation module. Contact **Product, Engineering, Partnerships, or Corporate Development**.

### Tier 3 — Labs, diagnostics, and patient-experience platforms

Position MediQuery as an acquisition-ready document organization/extraction component that can complement an existing patient or diagnostic workflow.

## Contact-channel sequence

For each target:

1. Warm introduction through a founder, investor, accelerator, MEDTEQ+, Montréal Invivo, Centech, or healthcare-technology network if available.
2. Direct executive email from the company's official contact channel.
3. LinkedIn connection/message to the target executive or product/technology leader.
4. Follow-up after 5–7 business days.
5. Final concise follow-up after another 7–10 business days.

Do not mass-mail all 20 at once. Run five-target batches and adapt the pitch based on replies.

## First message

Subject: Québec health-tech software asset — MediQuery

“Bonjour [Name],

Je vous contacte au sujet d’un actif logiciel health-tech que je développe et prépare actuellement pour une acquisition stratégique: MediQuery.

MediQuery fournit un workflow sécurisé de gestion de rapports médicaux PDF: authentification, isolation par utilisateur, extraction structurée de résultats de laboratoire, conservation de preuves par page et gestion/suppression des comptes. Le workflow principal est automatisé et couvert par des tests; le projet inclut également sa documentation de sécurité, d’architecture, de déploiement et de due diligence.

Je ne le présente pas comme un dispositif médical certifié ni comme une IA clinique. L’intérêt de l’acquisition est plutôt de donner à un acteur health-tech existant une base logicielle réutilisable et extensible.

Seriez-vous la bonne personne pour discuter d’une acquisition stratégique de ce type? Je peux envoyer un bref dossier technique et organiser une démonstration de 10 minutes.

Merci,
[Your name]”

## Qualification questions

Before discussing price, establish:

- Does the buyer already process medical documents or laboratory data?
- Do they have an existing patient/clinician portal?
- Would document extraction/evidence improve an existing product?
- Do they acquire software/IP or only build internally?
- Who owns technical and corporate-development decisions?
- What security/privacy and clinical review would be required?

## Price conversation

Do not lead with “this is worth $100K.” Lead with the strategic problem solved and the replacement/time-to-market value. If the buyer asks for an acquisition range, use the commercial package's **CAD $100,000+ opening ask** as a negotiation anchor, while making clear that final consideration depends on scope and diligence.

## Credibility rule

Never imply that a company has agreed to buy, evaluated MediQuery, endorsed it, or certified it unless that event actually occurs. Every target remains a prospect until there is documented engagement.
